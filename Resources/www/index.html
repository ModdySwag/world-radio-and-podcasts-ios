➕ Add a station

Anything that streams audio: a local station the catalogue is missing, a favourite that has gone, or a second, higher-quality stream of a station already listed.

How to: paste the stream address itself — the one that ends in .mp3, .aac, .ogg or .m3u8, or a bare address such as http://live.fresh927.com.au/freshmp3. A station page or a .pls/.m3u/.xspf playlist download will not play in a browser: open that playlist in a desktop player (VLC) and copy the address it points to. Test stream checks it here before you save; a station this browser cannot play is refused, and one that will not play everywhere is flagged. Your stations stay in this browser — nothing is uploaded.
